Explore the results

Two interactive tools on the test set (6,482 applicants the models never saw). Nothing is retrained: these are the frozen models' saved predictions.

Models used here
  • Reference Random Forest on details + grade + rate, isotonic-calibrated PD (nb 05, 04)
  • Pipeline A Random Forest on details + grade, isotonic-calibrated PD (nb 08); rate: Random Forest regressor on grade + details (nb 09)
  • Pipeline B Random Forest on details only, Platt-calibrated PD (nb 06); rate: Random Forest regressor on PD + details (nb 07)

1. Move the decision cutoff

An applicant is declined when their PD is at or above the cutoff. Lower it to catch more defaulters (but decline more good customers); raise it for the opposite. The dashed line marks the cutoff chosen on the training set.

2. Browse real test applicants

Filter, then click an applicant to see what each model decided, the suggested rates, and what really happened.
